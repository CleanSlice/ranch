# Contract: what a person sees

Customer console (`app`) only. The admin console gains no control and no text.

## Where the action is

| Screen | Place | Component |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Share page, `ready` state | right end of the page header | `<BridleChatNewChat :conversation="conversation" />` |
| Console agent chat | header actions, before Share | `<BridleChatNewChat :conversation="{ key: agent.id, agentId: agent.id }" />` |
| Landing page hero | — | not offered |
| Share page, `loading` / `invalid` | — | not rendered |

The component takes the conversation descriptor and nothing else; everything
it shows comes from the `bridle` store by key.

## The control

A text button with an icon, in the idiom of the header's Restart button.

| Store says (`newChatBlock`) | Button | Hint (tooltip and, on the share page, visible to assistive tech) |
|-----------------------------|--------|------|
| `null` | enabled | `chat.new_chat_hint` |
| `empty` | disabled | `chat.new_chat_empty` |
| `offline` | disabled | `chat.new_chat_offline` |
| `agent_offline` | disabled | `chat.new_chat_agent_offline` |
| `answering` | disabled | `chat.new_chat_answering` |
| `busy` | disabled, spinner | `chat.new_chat_starting` |

The reason is chosen in script and travels as a key (docs/i18n.md).

## Confirmation

Pressing the enabled button opens an inline confirmation anchored to it — the
same hand-rolled popover idiom as the share panel, no dialog primitive.

| Conversation | Sentence | Confirm | Cancel |
|--------------|----------|---------|--------|
| share | `chat.new_chat_confirm_share` | `chat.new_chat_yes` | `chat.new_chat_cancel` |
| console | `chat.new_chat_confirm_console` | `chat.new_chat_yes` | `chat.new_chat_cancel` |

Escape, an outside click and Cancel close it and change nothing. Confirm closes
it and starts the reset; the button shows `busy` until the answer.

## Outcome

| Outcome | What happens |
|---------|--------------|
| Success | The conversation shows its starting state; the composer is empty; no notice. |
| `409 AGENT_OFFLINE` | Conversation unchanged; notice `chat.new_chat_failed_agent_offline` |
| `409 TURN_IN_PROGRESS` | Conversation unchanged; notice `chat.new_chat_failed_answering` |
| `503`, network error, anything else | Conversation unchanged; notice `chat.new_chat_failed` |
| `403` on a share conversation | The share page's invalid-link state, through its existing interceptor; no notice |

Notices use the chat's existing dismissible error strip (`errors[key]`).

## History page (`/chats`)

- A two-state filter above the list: **Current** (default) and **Earlier**.
- **Earlier** lists the person's closed conversations, newest first; each card
  carries the `session.closed` marker.
- Opening a closed conversation shows it read-only, as the detail page already
  shows any conversation.
- The list renders from the `chat` store's `sessions`; the request supplies
  loading state only.

## Accessibility

- The button is a `<button>` with visible text; the disabled reason is in
  `title` and `aria-describedby`.
- The confirmation is focus-trapped while open, opens with focus on Cancel,
  and returns focus to the button on close.
- The `busy` state is announced (`aria-busy`).
